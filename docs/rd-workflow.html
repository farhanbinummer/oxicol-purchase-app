R&D Project Workflow

Oxicol Purchase App · from the boss's request to handover to Production

1. The journey

A. Request
Boss / Manager / Branch fills the R&D request form. Saved as a draft, then submitted.
Gets number OXRD011126 · status Received
→
B. R&D takes over
R&D sets the Project lead, trial estimate, feasibility and team, then auto-plans dates for all 8 stages.
→
C. Eight stages
Run in order. Each has a checklist; three need an outside sign-off.
→
D. Completed
After all 8 stages are done, R&D clicks Mark Project Completed.

2. The eight stages

Stage 1 · 7 daysLiterature / Raw-Material SourcingBenchmark TDS and SDS, shortlist materials and suppliers, request samples and quotes
Stage 2 · 5 daysFirst Lab BatchRecord formula and batch size, make the batch, check viscosity, solids, appearance
Stage 3 · 21 daysLab Trials & OptimisationRecord trials, compare with requirements, pick the formula, calculate cost
Stage 4 · 14 daysInternal Testing / QC ApprovalRun tests, compare with specs and benchmarksNeeds QC sign-off
Stage 5 · 3 daysSample to Requester / CustomerPack and label, include test summary or draft TDS, record dispatch
Stage 6 · 14 daysCustomer ApprovalWritten feedback, corrections and resubmissions, decision and evidenceNeeds customer evidence
Stage 7 · 7 daysPlant Trial / Scale-upPlant batch card, trial with Production, compare plant and lab QC
Stage 8 · 5 daysHandover to ProductionHand over formula, SOP and QC specs; safety, label and packaging documentsNeeds Production acknowledgement

To complete a stage: it must be the current one, every checklist item ticked, the sign-off recorded (where needed), a date that is not in the future, and the project not On Hold or Rejected. A stage can be reopened with a reason; later stages are then flagged for review.

3. Who is in charge

PersonRole in the project
Boss / ManagerStarts requests and sees all submitted projects. Cannot edit or complete stages by default.
RequesterWhoever starts the request (Boss, Manager or Branch staff). Can see their own projects.
R&D teamReviews the request, plans, runs stages, adds tasks and lab notes. Only R&D sees the confidential technical fields.
Project leadOne R&D person. Default owner of every stage that has no named owner.
Stage owner (optional)A named person for one particular stage.
QCGives the QC sign-off at stage 4.
Customer / OEMApproval evidence for stage 6 is recorded by an internal person.
ProductionAcknowledges the handover at stage 8; joins the plant trial at stage 7.
AdministratorCan do everything and decides who may record each approval.

4. How the work is divided

Stages
Belong to the Project lead, or a named stage owner.
Tasks
Smaller jobs inside a project. One accountable person each, or "Unclaimed (team)". Finishing a task never completes a stage.
Approvals
Recorded by the department that gives them, under their own name.
Comments & lab notes
The running discussion. Lab notes are confidential to R&D.

5. Gaps to know about

  • Nobody can record the sign-offs yet. No role has approval permissions by default. An administrator must grant them under Administration > Permissions, or stages 4, 6 and 8 cannot be completed.
  • No department hand-offs. Purchase, QC and Production are not notified or given work automatically. R&D creates tasks for them.
  • Status is changed by hand (Received, Under Review, Approved, In Lab Trials, Sample Sent, Plant Trial). Completing a stage does not change it.
  • No file attachments, and no email or WhatsApp messages for R&D events.
