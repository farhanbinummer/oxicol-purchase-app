Permissions

Tick or untick a box to grant or revoke that role's access to that action - it takes effect immediately, for everyone with that role, the next time they try it. Admin can always do everything and isn't shown as a checkbox, since that can't be turned off.

Not editable here, on purpose

User management (viewing, creating, renaming, or deleting accounts) and this permissions page itself always stay locked to admin, in the code, not in this table. If they were editable here, a mistake - or a compromised non-admin account - could grant itself admin-level power. Changing those two would need an actual code change, not a checkbox.

Notes on how roles are scoped

  • Branch logins only ever see and act on their own branch's requests - a Kannur login cannot view or approve a Thrissur request, even with "View requests" ticked for Branch.
  • Every signed-in user, of any role, can change their own password and log out (My Account) - that's not role-gated, so it isn't listed below.
  • Search results are automatically limited to what that role could open anyway.
